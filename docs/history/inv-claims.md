Correct wording for CSTF: "CSTF-aligned" is a self-declared benchmark. It is not an accreditation, endorsement or verification. Skills for Health does not assure commercial providers and the provider may not imply it does. Safe wording: "Course content is mapped to the Core Skills Training Framework learning outcomes. This is Vitalcare's own mapping and is not endorsed or verified by Skills for Health." The repo's own blog post (`src/data/blog.ts:21`, migration 051) already says "Alignment is not the same as accreditation", which contradicts how the same site then uses "accredited" next to "CSTF" on every page.

Correct wording for CPD: "CPD-accredited" means a named independent body (CPD Standards Office, or CPD Group / CPD Certification Service) has accredited that provider or that course, normally with a certificate and provider/course number visible in the body's directory. Without that, the only defensible phrase is "CPD hours are recorded" or "Counts towards CPD (self-certified)". The company is not found in the CPD Standards Office directory. Nothing in the repo, docs or git-visible files holds an accreditation certificate, number or accreditor name. `docs/LAUNCH-READINESS.md:200` already says "Nothing in this repository invents a renewal period, a pass mark or an accreditation claim, and nothing here should", and `docs/TEST-REPORT.md` lists "Accreditation claims, CPD hours and course content" as "not something a test can establish". The site nevertheless asserts CPD accreditation in roughly 40 places.

## 4. UK compliance risks in the wording

1. Advertising (CAP Code, ASA; also CMA consumer law). The accreditation, regulator and testimonial claims in section 2 fall under CAP rules on substantiation (3.7), genuine testimonials with documentary evidence and contact details (3.45-3.50), and approval/endorsement/trust-mark claims (3.52 by position) quoted in §1. "CPD-accredited", "CQC Compliant", "CQC-ready", the two hard-coded testimonials and "Verified feedback" are the highest-risk items. The Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 gives the CMA direct fining powers over misleading actions and fake reviews (ASSUMPTION from general knowledge; not fetched in this audit). Business-to-business advertising is covered by the Business Protection from Misleading Marketing Regulations 2008 (ASSUMPTION).
2. Refund page and Consumer Contracts Regulations. `RefundPolicyPage.tsx:20-23`: "full refund before you first access the course content ... once you have begun the course, the fee is non-refundable". For a consumer buying at a distance, the CCR 2013 gives a 14-day cancellation right for services and for digital content; the right for digital content is lost only if the consumer gave prior express consent to supply starting inside the 14 days and acknowledged losing the right (§1). The policy makes "first access" the trigger with no consent or acknowledgement step (confirmed by `docs/LAUNCH-READINESS.md:227`, "the consent flow that requires was not established", and `docs/PRIVACY-DATA-MAP.md` legal counsel item 2). It also states no 14-day cancellation right, no model cancellation form or "14 days from the day of the contract" statement, and sets the refund time as "within 14 days" of an approved request rather than 14 days from notice of cancellation. Live-session refund tiers (14+ days full, 7-13 days 50 percent, under 7 none) are contractual terms that sit alongside, and cannot override, the statutory right for consumers. Employer/B2B bookings are outside the CCR, but the Terms do not separate the two. `TermsPage.tsx:15` also self-certifies compliance with the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
3. Privacy notice versus actual data flows (`PrivacyPolicyPage.tsx`, marked "DRAFT PENDING LEGAL SIGN-OFF" in its own header comment, lines 1-14). Checked against `docs/PRIVACY-DATA-MAP.md` and the code:
   - ICO number "available on request" (A8): unconfirmed. If a fee is owed and unpaid, the policy and `ai-chat` brief both mislead.
   - Cloudflare is not named. The live site is served through Cloudflare (`/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection` links on https://vitalcare.uk/; DNS/HTTPS per your standard). It sees every visitor's IP. The policy names a generic "web hosting provider" only. The contact-form edge function also calls Cloudflare Turnstile (`supabase/functions/contact-form/index.ts:98`, `_shared/turnstile.ts`), but the contact form UI sends no token (`ContactPage.tsx:49-57`). Today that is harmless because verification is skipped when no `TURNSTILE_SECRET` is configured; if a secret is ever set, every enquiry will be rejected, and if Turnstile is adopted the policy must name Cloudflare as a processor.
   - Supabase region, transfer safeguards per provider (Resend, Google, Zoom, OpenRouter, OpenWeather) and retention for compliance, communications, uploads, payroll and audit logs are all "REQUIRES OWNER INPUT" in the data map (open items 2-5). The policy states retention for training (7 y), contact (3 y), financial (6 y) and marketing consent but is silent on the rest.
   - It claims "Card details are never entered into or stored by this platform" and names no payment processor: consistent with manual bank/PayPal-label payments (`constants.ts` PAYMENT). The store offers PayPal as a label only (`LAUNCH-READINESS.md:179`).
   - Data processed by an AI writing assistant (Google AI / OpenRouter) is disclosed. Good.
   - Hosting claim "UK" for the cPanel host is in the data map but is not stated in the policy; unverified.
   - "No special category data" statement is true of the schema; the emergency-contact section is good practice.
   - Legal basis for the 7-year retention is not evidenced (G12). `docs/PRIVACY-DATA-MAP.md` calls rights routes manual; one-month response time is an operational commitment.
4. Cookie banner versus actual cookies and trackers. `CookiePolicyPage.tsx` says no banner is needed and that browsing the public site sets no cookies. Evidence offered: `tests/e2e/privacy.spec.ts` (I read its host list, not a live run, and did not run the test). Repo evidence agrees: no analytics, tag manager, pixel, recaptcha, Sentry or replay anywhere in `src/`, `index.html` or `package.json` (grep). Third parties contacted on every public page: Google Fonts (`index.html:21-34`) and Unsplash (`marketing-images.ts`), which receive visitor IPs, disclosed in the policy. Residual points: (a) `sidebar_state` cookie and theme/notification local-storage keys are classed "preference", and a PECR exemption is strongest only for strictly necessary storage, so a cautious reading may want consent or a clearer "user-initiated" argument (ASSUMPTION; PECR changes introduced by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 not checked); (b) Cloudflare may set its own cookies/challenges on the live host (`/cdn-cgi/`), which the policy does not list; (c) WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Instagram are outbound links only. Fonts and photography could be self-hosted to remove the transfers entirely, which is open item 6 in the data map.
5. Other: `index.html` noscript text and `manifest.webmanifest:4` carry "CSTF-aligned, CPD-accredited" and so propagate C2 into install prompts and search snippets. Sitemap lists 75 course URLs that resolve from a static fallback (D4). `PageMeta.tsx:25` default description repeats the claim on every page without its own description.
